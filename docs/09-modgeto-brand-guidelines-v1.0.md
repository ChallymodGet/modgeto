## 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic, verbal, and visual identity of Modgeto. It is the source of truth for product copy, marketing communication, public-facing pages, transactional emails, onboarding, UI terminology, brand assets, and future campaign work.

Modgeto must feel like a serious professional tool built for creative people. It should never drift into the language or conventions of a low-cost freelance marketplace, generic project-management application, or ecommerce platform.

# 3. What Modgeto Is

## One-Line Definition

> Modgeto gives Creatives one personalized link where Clients can choose a Service, configure what they need, complete the Project Brief, understand the price, and get ready to start working together.

## Short Product Description

> Modgeto helps designers, developers, writers and other digital Creatives turn Client interest into structured Project Requests. Set up your Services, pricing, deliverables, Briefs and working terms once, then share your Modgeto link wherever Clients already find you.

## Positioning Statement

Creatives should not have to explain the same Services, prices, deliverables, timelines and Project requirements every time someone says, “I'd like to work with you.”

Modgeto brings that process into one personalized link. Creatives define how they work, Clients configure what they need and provide the right information upfront, and both sides reach a clear starting point before the Project begins.

# 9. Brand Personality

Modgeto is defined by five characteristics.

## Clear

Modgeto turns ambiguity into structure. Its language should do the same.

**Avoid:**

> Facilitate frictionless Client engagement experiences.

**Use:**

> Give Clients everything they need to start a Project with you.

## Confident

Modgeto should sound certain without becoming arrogant.

**Avoid:**

> We believe we may be able to help improve your Client intake.

**Use:**

> Set your Services once. Share your link wherever Clients find you.

## Human

Write for people rather than database entities.

**Avoid:**

> Request entity successfully instantiated.

**Use:**

> Your Request has been sent to Charles.

## Creative

Personality should come through in strong phrasing, thoughtful interfaces, expressive imagery, and small moments of warmth rather than constant jokes, slang, emoji, or gimmicks.

## Professional

Modgeto may display Projects worth hundreds of thousands or millions of naira. Commercial moments should always feel credible, calm, and trustworthy.

---

# 10. Brand Archetype

## Primary: Creator

Modgeto empowers people who make things and provides a system through which they can package their professional value.

## Secondary: Sage

Modgeto brings clarity, structure, and informed decisions to what is often an ambiguous Client relationship.

Modgeto is not primarily a Jester or Ruler brand. It should neither lead with humour nor attempt to control how Creatives operate.

# 11. Verbal Identity

## Three-Word Filter

> **Clear. Capable. Creative.**

Future brand communication should pass these three tests:

1. Is it easy to understand?
2. Does it sound like a competent professional product?
3. Does it retain enough creative personality to belong to its users?

## Voice Summary

> **Warm, concise, confident and professional with a creative edge.**

# 12. Voice Principles

## 12.1 Say What Happens Next

**Avoid:**

> Your submission has been processed.

**Use:**

> Your Request has been sent to Charles.

Follow with useful context:

> Charles will review your scope and let you know if anything needs to change.

## 12.2 Use the User's Language

Preferred words:

- Service
- Client
- Brief
- Project
- Price
- Commitment
- Request

Avoid unnecessary SaaS/internal language such as:

- Pipeline object
- Conversion entity
- Engagement unit
- Intake artifact

## 12.3 Make Money Language Explicit

Never imply that Modgeto has processed or verified external Project payments.

**Avoid:**

> Payment successful.

**Use:**

> You've told Charles you've made the payment.

Then:

> Awaiting confirmation.

Creative-facing:

> Confirm the amount you actually received.

## 12.4 Do Not Sound Accusatory

**Avoid:**

> Your payment is incorrect.

**Use:**

> Charles confirmed receiving ₦100,000.

> ₦50,000 remains to complete your commitment.

## 12.5 Keep Important Moments Calm

**Avoid:**

> Start Project!!!!!! 🎉🚀

**Use:**

> Ready to start

> Everything required to begin this Project is complete.

**Start Project**

# 14. Capitalisation and Writing Style

Use important concepts naturally in customer-facing copy:

- Create a Service.
- Submit your Project Request.
- Complete the Brief.

Internal documentation and domain models may use formal capitalization consistently.

## Writing Rules

- Use contractions where natural.
- Keep sentences reasonably short.
- Front-load the meaning.
- Write buttons as actions.
- Use specific nouns.
- Avoid jargon.
- Avoid unnecessary exclamation marks.
- Avoid repeated punctuation.
- Do not make emoji part of the core product voice.

# 24. Error Voice

Errors should explain:

1. What happened
2. What was preserved
3. What the user can do now

## File Upload

### We couldn't upload this file

Your other Brief answers are saved.

**Try Again**

## Service Unavailable

### This Service isn't available right now

Charles isn't currently accepting new Requests for this Service.

**View Other Services**

Do not expose private reasons such as the Creative's subscription status.

Approximate balance:

- 80% neutral
- 15% Primary Indigo
- 5% semantic/Secondary/Tertiary accents

# 37. Brand Gradient

Approved gradient:

```css
linear-gradient(
  135deg,
  #635BFF 0%,
  #8B80FF 45%,
  #06B6D4 100%
)
```

Use sparingly for brand artwork, onboarding visuals, and selected decorative moments.

Do not use it throughout routine dashboards, forms, navigation, tables, or every CTA.

# 40. Radius

- Small: 6px
- Medium: 8px
- Large: 12px
- XL: 16px

# 43. Logo Direction

## Primary Wordmark Direction

Explore the lowercase wordmark:

> **modgeto**

The lowercase treatment supports a modern, approachable professional identity.

## Symbol Territory

Explore abstract ideas around:

- Links
- Connection
- Structured Service blocks
- Creative-to-Client handoff
- The letter `m`
- A forward/start gesture

Avoid literal symbols such as:

- Briefcase
- Handshake
- Dollar sign
- Shopping cart
- Generic freelancer avatar

## Colour Direction

Primary wordmark applications should use Neutral 900 and/or Primary Indigo. The symbol may selectively use an Indigo-to-Cyan relationship where appropriate.

The logo mark itself is not yet finalized by this document.
